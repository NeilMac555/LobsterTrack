## Act maps (`map1.png`–`map5.png`)

Walls, paving, water, lava, bog, statues, altars, fountains, gateways and the
secrets' treasures (Biski's cat statue, the golden idol, the goldfish, the
quokka, chests and gold) are **Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup** tiles, **CC0**
(public domain): https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles —
picked and packed into one 32px sheet per act. The desert's "X" is DCSS's
diagonal sigil laid over its mirror image.

The jungle's bridges (plank decks and railings in `map3.png`) are "LPC style
wood bridges" by **Xenodora**, **CC-BY-SA 3.0** / GPL 3.0 / GPL 2.0:
https://opengameart.org/node/15135 — so `map3.png` (and `hd/map3.png`) is
shared under **CC-BY-SA 3.0**.

## HD sheets (`hd/`)

`hd/<name>.png` is a 2x version of `<name>.png` for the HD graphics mode; each
keeps the licence and credits of its 1x sheet listed in this file (the hero
sheets therefore stay **CC-BY-SA 3.0**). Most are upscaled with the **xBR**
pixel-art scaling algorithm (by Hyllian; applied offline with the MIT-licensed
xBRjs by Josep del Rio, https://github.com/joseprio/xBRjs — no new colours
created, floor tiles scaled as wrapping tiles so they stay seamless). Where the
1x sheet had shrunk larger source art, the HD sheet is rebuilt from that source
instead: the skill icons (Bjerk's 256px paintings), Neil/Zoe/Ana (64px LPC
frames), and the fire skull, devilkin and shark.
