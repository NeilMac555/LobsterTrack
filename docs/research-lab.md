# Research Lab

The Tools menu opens /tools/research-lab. The fixed public report covers English clubs at home in Europe in 2026/27. The member workspace accepts bounded football questions. Parsing, source selection and settlement run on the server. Asking from an existing report can change its venue, price limit, last-game window or European competition.

## Direction contract
Inherit the SteamWatch research tools: navy, slate and cyan, existing site type and tabular prices. The primary desktop and mobile action is entering a question. The answer puts the interpreted scope and four numbers above the competition breakdown; the match ledger opens in place. Keep the football record separate from the bet count. The public report uses the same renderer. Saved questions compare their baseline with the new full UTC match days after saving.

## Coverage
Domestic results use the named club’s archived season and league. The nine English entrants in 2026/27 use Sportmonks 90-minute results, plus the two verified Brighton qualifier legs. Results remain eligible when a price is unavailable. SteamWatch prices must match the original pre-match Pinnacle snapshot and be recorded in the final five minutes. Domestic archival prices explicitly tagged as Pinnacle closes and the two cited qualifying prices are also accepted. Do not substitute opening odds, exchange prices or estimates. One unit is staked per priced selection. If two qualifying English teams meet, backing each one is two selections.

Tracking fixes the season and rules, stores the summary at save time and counts qualifying results from the next UTC date. A last-N window defines the discovery sample, not a cap on forward results. Access to arbitrary runs and all saved-question endpoints requires active Pro membership.

Members can also ask which teams steamed or drifted most across the five domestic leagues or in one named league. These rankings use the same opening prices, 3pp minimum and final two-hour observation window as Steam Results and Drifters. Count the largest qualifying move once per team per fixture, whether or not it was alerted or settled. Rank by count, share places on ties, and show pending results separately. Each row has a fixture ledger. A league-only follow-up retains the direction and season. Saved forward tracking applies to win-bet reports.
